This should be a private management platform, not a public patient portal. Patients should continue using GEN-ART's website and approved communication channels; the dashboard should consolidate de-identified business intelligence and authorized operational information.

2. Seven essential modules

Module 1 — Global Market Opportunity Intelligence

Identify countries with the strongest potential to send patients to Ankara. Evaluate infertility treatment demand, affordability, IVF availability, cross-border travel, competition, and GEN-ART's existing patient history.

AI should recommend countries and explain why they are attractive, not simply rank countries by population.

Module 2 — International Patient CRM

Track the complete journey: inquiry received → qualified → consultation → medical assessment → treatment plan → travel confirmed → treatment started → follow-up.

Include contact source, preferred language, coordinator, next action, response time, reason for cancellation, and follow-up status. Keep sensitive medical details within the authorized clinical record system.

Module 3 — Digital Marketing & Patient Discovery

Integrate Google Analytics 4, Search Console, Google Ads, Meta campaign results, and approved inquiry channels.

Identify which countries, languages, search terms, informational pages, and campaigns generate actual treatment starts rather than just clicks.

Module 4 — AI Forecasting & Scenario Simulation

Estimate future inquiries, qualified leads, treatment starts, acquisition cost, revenue, and capacity requirements.

Simulate scenarios such as increasing the Arabic-language campaign budget, launching a Russian-language information page, or improving the consultation conversion rate.

Module 5 — International Patient Travel & Treatment Coordination

Track visa requirements, direct or connecting flights, likely travel barriers, accommodation arrangements, and expected treatment stay.

Provide operational coordination without automatically predicting medical eligibility or treatment duration.

Module 6 — Regulatory & Ethical Compliance

Monitor Turkish health-tourism promotion rules, country-specific treatment restrictions, data protection, appropriate consent, and approved marketing content.

Separate marketing permissions from clinical consent. All treatment eligibility decisions remain with qualified healthcare professionals.

Module 7 — Executive Financial & Performance Intelligence

Compare patient acquisition cost, consultation conversion, treatment revenue, contribution margin, country profitability, and marketing return.

Include financial forecasts and explanations of why performance improved or declined.

7. Regulatory and privacy safeguards

This part needs stronger controls than your ZEUS commercial dashboard.

Türkiye's 26 April 2025 international health tourism regulation sets operating requirements for authorized healthcare providers, and the 12 November 2025 promotion regulation imposes specific conditions on overseas-facing health tourism communication. GEN-ART's campaigns must be reviewed against those requirements before launch. 

Resmi Gazete+1

Also, IVF and infertility information can constitute sensitive health data under Türkiye's KVKK framework. The dashboard should use role-based access, secure clinical-system separation, data minimization, retention rules, and documented lawful processing grounds. 

Kişisel Verileri Koruma Kurumu+1

I would implement a campaign compliance checklist and a mandatory human approval stage for proposed advertisements and clinical claims.

4. One important improvement over ZEUS

I would add a feature called AI Patient Acquisition Opportunity Engine.

It would connect three questions:

Which countries show meaningful demand for GEN-ART's services?

Which countries produce qualified patients at sustainable acquisition costs?

Which barriers prevent interested patients from beginning treatment?

The engine should propose a strategy, calculate projected outcomes, and allow management to challenge the assumptions through your existing human–AI decision-support approach.

For example, it could compare whether investing in Arabic-language patient education, improving website conversion, or increasing a particular country's advertising budget offers the best expected return.
